Template for writing a new subunit class.

Save as subunits/<Name>.py - the subunits package imports it automatically.

`aliases` is the list of names the user can type after --subunit/-s. They are
matched case-insensitively and ignoring spaces and underscores, so "hollow
sphere", "Hollow_Sphere" and "hollowsphere" all resolve to the same class.
The first alias is the canonical name and is the one used in error messages.

A subunit describes its body in its own frame, centred at the origin. Where the
body is placed (com, rotation, rotation_point) and its SLD are handled by the
Subunit base class, so pass them on with super().__init__(**properties).

The points of a model are cut out of a static cloud: every point of the cloud
inside the body, as decided by checkOverlap(), takes the SLD of the subunit.
So checkOverlap() alone defines the body, and the other two methods must agree
with it:
 - getHalfWidths() must describe a box that encloses the whole body. Points
   outside the box are never tested, and the cloud extends beyond it, so
   checkOverlap() must also reject every point outside the box.
 - getVolume() must return the volume of the body itself - not of the box. It
   sets the density of the cloud.
